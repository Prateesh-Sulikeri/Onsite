      Privacy

      Plain terms for what Onsite does with your data

      What stays on this computer

      • Your resume, job descriptions, transcripts, code, whiteboards, feedback, dossiers and settings, in Onsite’s database in your app data folder.
      • Your voice. The built-in voice engine, VoiceStudio and Whisper all run on this computer; audio is never uploaded by Onsite.
      • API keys, stored in the same local database. They are not encrypted, so anyone with access to your Windows account can read them. Remove a key in AI model when you stop using it.
      • The log file, which holds errors and timings, not transcripts or keys.

      What leaves this computer

      • To the AI you connect (Claude Code, the Claude API, OpenAI, Gemini or Groq): the interview prompt, your resume and job description, what you say or type, and your code and whiteboard when you share them. Feedback, the resume check and research send the same kind of data. That provider’s own privacy policy and data settings apply.
      • Research jobs ask that provider to search the web. The company, role and job description you enter are part of the request.
      • Voice models (built-in engine) download once from github.com when you press Download in Settings. GitHub sees your IP address. Speech itself never leaves this computer.
      • Update checks ask GitHub for the release lists of Onsite and VoiceStudio, only when you press Check for updates. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website.

      What Onsite never does

      • No accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
      • No data is sent to the Onsite developer.
      • The interviewer gets no tools: it can’t browse, run commands or read your files.
      • Model replies and dossiers are shown and saved as text, never run as code.

      Deleting your data

      • Delete single rounds from Feedback, dossiers from Dossiers, and old rounds automatically with the limits in Storage.
      • Uninstalling Onsite leaves your data folder in place. Delete it from Storage → Open data folder to remove everything.
